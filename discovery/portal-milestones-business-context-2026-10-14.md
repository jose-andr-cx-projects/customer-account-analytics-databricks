## Analytics use

- Organise the CX experience analysis by **Access & Identity; Understand & Prepare; Apply & Submit; Track & Respond; Outcome & Manage**. Milestones annotate timelines and explain potential changes in service mix; product/service categories remain secondary comparison filters.
- **Pre-2025 events provide historical business context**, not a claim of complete CDH event coverage. Source-system records may date earlier than 2025, but each CDH metric needs verified source/event history availability.
- April 2025 residential parking migration and CaseHistory start (reported 2025-04-10) overlap. Treat the launch period and potential migrations separately from steady-state experience. Do not interpret missing history as zero FIR events.
- The reported 16,500 and 29,600 transaction values lack explicitly defined reporting intervals, inclusion rules and source measurement definitions in the milestone table. **Do not use them as reconciled monthly KPI values or calculate growth rates without confirming the basis.**
- The dates may be useful reference annotations for descriptive trend analysis. Do not claim any milestone caused an observed volume, friction or customer-effort change without compatible cohorts and supporting evidence.
- Before comparing FIR or repeated-FIR rates between Business/Construction and residential parking, verify service classification, source coverage, submission eligibility, start-date and follow-up window, and migration/staff-created record handling.
- Keep events and status history within their observable period, and retain the distinction between a Case event, BLA application, communication message and customer experience.
